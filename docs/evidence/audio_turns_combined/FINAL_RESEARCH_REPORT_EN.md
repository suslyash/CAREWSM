## Interpretation

The feature screen shows that the strongest compact candidates were AB, A, and ABC by VAL, while the scheduled multi-family survivors were ABCD, ABC, AB, and AC. Gated fusion improved the corresponding ABCD, ABC, and AC/AB mean-pooling baselines in this batch, with ABCD the strongest gated candidate. Adding mean+std did not improve ABCD over gated mean, although its fold variability was lower. Hidden dimension and architecture mattered: the hidden-64 gated model was much stronger than the hidden-64 residual MLP. Among MTL strategies, Equal had the highest VAL mean; PCGrad and STCH were close but lower, and DB-MTL was lower in this new turnless-mean batch despite strong historical native-turn evidence. TEST means are descriptive and do not establish significance.
