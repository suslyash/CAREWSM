\subsection{Five-seed DEV evidence}

Table~\ref{tab:main} gives the frozen primary comparison. Both multimodal finalists exceed temporal audio Mean\_Score on all five DEV seeds. The paired DEV Mean difference for full R4$-$audio is $+0.024912$ ($[+0.001169,+0.048655]$), and for shared$-$audio it is $+0.025769$ ($[+0.009556,+0.041984]$). The shared$-$R4 difference is $+0.000858$ ($[-0.009704,+0.011419]$), so the two multimodal finalists are not separated by the predeclared paired DEV interval. This is the DEV-only basis for retaining the equal-parameter shared model as the parsimonious primary candidate and full R4 as the secondary multimodal reference.

\subsection{Task trade-off rather than a single undifferentiated gain}

The aggregate Mean can conceal a disease-specific trade-off. On TEST\_NONE, shared fusion raises depression Score from .752954 (audio) to .799233, a $+.046279$ difference, while Parkinson Score falls from .848230 to .812774, a $-.035456$ difference; the resulting Mean difference is only $+.005411$. The same broad pattern appears in the final summaries: audio is particularly strong for Parkinson, whereas A+V fusion improves depression more consistently and can give back Parkinson performance. This is why D/P/Mean are reported together and why a small aggregate Test difference is not used to revise the frozen roles.

\subsection{Internal video evidence and external DEPART context}

The two internal DEPART-like video models exhibit a related task trade-off (Table~\ref{tab:video}). V2 was selected over V1 by the predeclared DEV Mean rule: V2$-$V1 is $-.041822$ for depression, $+.048417$ for Parkinson, and $+.003298$ for Mean. At the same DEV-selected epoch, V2's Test Mean monitoring values are lower than V1's on NONE, SOFT, and HARD. Those Test values did not drive family selection and are not a retrospective reversal of the DEV-only decision.

\subsection{Stage-6 mechanism record}

Table~\ref{tab:ablations} separates the frozen quantitative comparisons from their narrow conclusions. Direct pseudo-supervision and uncertainty/reliability have supported claims under their exact gates, although the latter effect is small. The no-audio result supports the online-audio-input contribution under the unchanged pseudo-training pipeline. By contrast, no-video fails its predeclared contribution criterion despite a lower aggregate ablation Mean, because the disease-specific condition fails. The model still contains RA-STCH, auxiliary heads, and agreement in the frozen final recipe; their inclusion should not be read as an unqualified necessity claim.
