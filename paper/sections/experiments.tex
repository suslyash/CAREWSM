\section{Frozen Experimental Design}
\label{sec:experiments}

The final compared set was locked before Test reporting: temporal audio, full R4 trial012, and equal-parameter shared fusion, each with seeds 42--46. For every run, the selected checkpoint is the epoch with maximum DEV Mean\_Score; the selected checkpoint identity was then frozen before the final evaluator read any same-seed Test outputs. The one-shot evaluator consumed the 15 predeclared checkpoint/config identities under the frozen reporting contract. No threshold fitting, recalibration, retry, architecture modification, or role revision followed final Test reporting.

\paragraph{Frozen roles.}
Shared fusion is the primary parsimonious paper candidate. Full R4 trial012 is the secondary multimodal reference, and frozen temporal audio is the baseline. This assignment was a DEV-only parsimony decision: shared and full R4 have equal parameter count and their paired DEV Mean interval did not separate them. It is not a Final-Test ranking or superiority statement.

\paragraph{Comparisons and summaries.}
Primary metrics are segment-level disease Scores and Mean\_Score. The predeclared same-seed paired summaries are full R4$-$audio, shared$-$audio, and shared$-$R4. We show their frozen means and descriptive 95\% intervals, but do not reinterpret them as multiplicity-corrected statistical tests. Test views are TEST\_NONE, TEST\_SOFT, and TEST\_HARD; they are all reports of the same frozen model set.

\paragraph{Calibration diagnostic.}
Calibration is DEV-only and diagnostic. It uses observed rows only (621 depression and 312 Parkinson rows per seed), sigmoid probabilities, 15 equal-width bins, Brier score, and ECE-15. No recalibration, threshold search, or class balancing is applied. Table~\ref{tab:calibration} copies the accepted five-seed summaries rather than re-estimating any value.

\paragraph{Text and description context.}
T1 is a completed standalone transcript baseline using frozen XLM-R features. Its three-seed DEV D/P/Mean is 0.6268063333/0.8447246667/0.7357653333, with visibly weaker and more seed-sensitive depression performance. It is not evidence that text improves A+V fusion. The planned T2 transcript-plus-observable-description family stopped before cache construction, model implementation, or training: its frozen preflight found demographic/identity inference in 4/24 outputs and judged only 18/24 outputs concise enough. T2 therefore has no performance claim.
