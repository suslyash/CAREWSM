\section{Reproducibility and Evidence Traceability}
\label{app:reproducibility}

This appendix records the frozen identities and accepted evidence used by the manuscript. It is a traceability supplement, not a new experiment or analysis. The final roles remain unchanged: equal-parameter shared fusion is the primary parsimonious candidate by the pre-Test DEV-only decision, full R4 trial012 is the secondary multimodal reference, and temporal audio is the baseline. The one-shot Final Test did not revise these roles.
